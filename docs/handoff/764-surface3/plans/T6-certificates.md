## RULE S1 (shared, 6 files, 7 sites) — the section-header icon chip

The single highest-leverage edit of the group: every form section opens with a coloured 32px
icon chip, one hue per section (amber Design, violet Issuer, emerald Signature, blue Certificate
info, rose Completion criteria, orange Expiration).

From (verbatim shape, `<hue>` differs per file):

```tsx
<div className="w-8 h-8 rounded-lg bg-<hue>-500/10 flex items-center justify-center">
    <Icon<X> className="h-4 w-4 text-<hue>-600 dark:text-<hue>-400" />
</div>
```

To:

```tsx
<div className="w-8 h-8 rounded-lg bg-brand-tint flex items-center justify-center">
    <Icon<X> className="h-4 w-4 text-brand-text" />
</div>
```

Sites:

- `design-section.tsx` **lines 22–23** (basic-tier locked branch) **and lines 39–40** (custom branch) — both, identically.
- `issuer-section.tsx` lines 17–18
- `signature-section.tsx` lines 17–18
- `certificate-info-section.tsx` lines 17–18
- `completion-criteria-section.tsx` lines 17–18
- `expiration-section.tsx` lines 17–18

Why:

- The six hues are decoration (a different colour per section of one form), not meaning. Mapping
  them by hue would stamp `warning` on Design, `success` on Signature, `destructive` on Completion
  criteria and `warning` on Expiration — four false status signals on a plain settings form.
  Status tokens are reserved for real states.
- `brand-text` on `brand-tint` is the AA-safe brand pair; never `text-primary` on a tint.
- The `dark:` half is deleted: `brand-text` / `brand-tint` already carry both modes.
- The heading beside the chip (`text-sm … text-muted-foreground/70`) is already tokenised — leave it.

Net: −21 palette (design 6, other five files 3 each).
See judgment call **T6-certificates-2** — the orchestrator may prefer a neutral `bg-muted` /
`text-muted-foreground` chip instead; the executor applies whichever it picks to all 7 sites.


   `hover:bg-muted/50`, `focus-visible:ring-primary`, `bg-muted`, `bg-muted/20`) — leave it.
